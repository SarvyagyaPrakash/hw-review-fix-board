# Taxonomy v1.0

Frozen taxonomy of 16 actionable review themes mapped across Hitwicket’s 9 functional teams.

| # | Theme ID | Display Name | Definition | Owning Teams (Primary First) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `onboarding_confusion` | Onboarding Confusion | Could not understand basic game rules, auction mechanics, or squad management upon starting. | product-analyst, ui-ux-designer, product-associate |
| 2 | `tutorial_length` | Tutorial Length & Flow | Tutorial is perceived as overly long, tedious, unskippable, or forced on experienced players. | product-associate, growth-analyst |
| 3 | `auction_issues` | Auction Mechanics | Auction bidding timing, player availability, bidding bot suspicion, or auction UI lag. | game-developer, ui-ux-designer, product-associate |
| 4 | `lag_performance` | Lag & Performance | Low frame rate, screen stuttering, device overheating, battery drain, or slow animations. | software-engineer, data-analyst |
| 5 | `crashes_bugs` | Crashes & Bugs | App crashes to home screen, black/white frozen screens, or stuck match states requiring restart. | software-engineer, product-analyst |
| 6 | `pay_to_win` | Pay To Win Sentiment | Perception that real-money spending decides match outcomes or grants insurmountable advantages. | game-developer, product-analyst, founders-office |
| 7 | `matchmaking_balance` | Matchmaking Balance | Unfair match pairings, opponents having vastly higher level or squad rating, or abrupt difficulty spikes. | data-analyst, game-developer |
| 8 | `pricing_and_shop` | Pricing & Shop UX | In-app purchases perceived as overpriced, confusing currencies, accidental purchases, or shop layout issues. | ui-ux-designer, product-associate, data-analyst |
| 9 | `ads_notifications` | Ads & Notifications | Excessive ad frequency, unskippable interstitial ads, ads crashing the game, or spammy push notifications. | growth-analyst, marketing-associate, product-associate |
| 10 | `support_refunds` | Support & Refunds | Customer support not responding, money deducted without item credit, or refund requests ignored. | founders-office, product-associate, data-analyst |
| 11 | `localisation` | Localisation & Regional | Missing regional languages (Hindi, Tamil, Telugu), poor translations, or cultural commentary. | marketing-associate, product-associate |
| 12 | `social_features` | Social & Multiplayer | Issues with friends lists, direct match invites, clan/alliance features, or chat. | product-associate, growth-analyst, marketing-associate |
| 13 | `release_quality` | Release & Update Quality | Problems, bugs, or regression introduced directly after a recent version update. | software-engineer, founders-office |
| 14 | `progression_grind` | Progression & Grind | Upgrade pacing is overly slow, repetitive matches, drought of upgrade cards, or stamina depletion. | game-developer, product-analyst |
| 15 | `account_data_loss` | Account & Data Loss | Login failures (Google Play Games, Facebook), lost team progress, or guest account wipes. | software-engineer, founders-office |
| 16 | `other` | Other Actionable Feedback | Specific, actionable feedback that does not cleanly fit into any of the predefined 15 categories. | product-analyst |

## Seed Cross-Check & Verification
- All 9 functional teams own at least one theme.
- Every theme maps to 1–3 teams.
- `other` is strictly the last entry (#16).
